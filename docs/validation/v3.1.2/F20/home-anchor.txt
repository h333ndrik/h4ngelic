Final production-preview rerun after F18-F27:
PASS SEO live interiors and Home Contact: direct/refresh routes work without
Three.js scene, font or reflection requests.

Root Home still activates the hero preload template and fetches the hero chunks.
A direct /#contact anchor now stays cold until the hero approaches the viewport,
because prepareHero activates preloads only when the initial fragment resolves to
the hero (or no fragment is present). The IntersectionObserver also activates the
template immediately before importing main.js when the hero later becomes visible.

The production preload link now resolves to the emitted dynamic hero chunk
(`main-*.js`) instead of the raw `/src/main.js` copy. Vite was copying that href
as an untransformed raw asset, so it could not preload the actual Three.js chunk
and broke the offline export. The build plugin rewrites the template href and
removes the duplicate raw asset. Verified in dist: index.html and the app bundle
both reference the same `main-*.js`, and only that file remains in assets.
